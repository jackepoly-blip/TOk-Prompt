## Prompt

Use this prompt with your uploaded product photo. 
The tool will extract the product identity exactly, then design a single premium advertising image where the art direction is bold, imaginative, and aligned with the brand’s essence. The goal is not to repeat generic effects or overcomplicate, but to create Behance-quality campaign visuals that feel custom-designed for the specific product and its audience, balancing photographic realism with conceptual artistry.
Image Prompt:
[Attach an image of the product you want to feature]
Then paste this prompt in the chat along with your uploaded image...
Analyze the subject in the attached image and generate a single, campaign-quality advertising image featuring it as the clear hero. Begin by analyzing the product with complete precision: study its geometry, proportions, finishes, surface details, labels, and colors, and preserve these flawlessly so the recreated product is perfectly identical to the original. Ignore the crop, lighting, and background of the uploaded photo — those are irrelevant. The uploaded product serves identity only. The generated result must feel like a reimagined editorial visual, original and art-directed, with the finish and creativity of a Behance-featured campaign. The philosophy is to design adaptively and intelligently. The AI must interpret not only what the product is, but what it represents, what values it projects, and who it is meant to inspire. Each image should express the unique story, benefit, and emotional tone of the product through its environment, mood, and artistic treatment. This requires freedom to blend photographic craft with digital creativity. The foundation should feel like a real photo — grounded in believable lighting, perspective, and composition — but the environment, accents, and visual language can extend beyond realism to symbolically amplify the product’s vibe. If the product expresses boldness, the scene might integrate dynamic geometry, contrast-driven lighting, or abstract design forms that reinforce energy. If it conveys calm, refinement, or natural purity, the scene might use softer gradients, atmospheric layers, or minimal artistic accents. The point is not to insert arbitrary visual effects, but to craft a visual system that embodies the product’s essence. Composition must always highlight the product as the dominant focal point. However, the AI should have creative freedom with framing, scale, and perspective: zooming in to highlight fine detail, pulling back to situate the product in a striking environment, shifting angles to evoke power, elegance, intimacy, or motion. Each variation should feel like a different professional interpretation of the same product campaign, avoiding repetition. Lighting must act as a storytelling device, sculpting dimensionality and atmosphere. It may be soft and diffuse, sharp and directional, dramatic with strong contrast, or glowing with subtle reflections — but it must always feel intentional, in service of brand emotion. Color grading should unify the product with its environment, extending the palette in ways that emphasize mood while keeping the product authentic. Supporting design elements should emerge as conceptual art direction, never random clutter. These can include textures, abstract shapes, motion-inspired lines, or symbolic accents that communicate product benefits and elevate emotion. Everything must feel purposeful, enhancing storytelling while keeping the product at the center. Post-processing should refine the final result to Behance-level polish: sharpness, depth, tonal richness, clean contrast, and subtle artistic finishing touches that unify realism with concept-driven design. The result must be a single striking image — never multiple panels, never gimmicky, and never busy for its own sake. Variety across outputs should emerge from experimentation in angle, crop, lighting, color tone, and conceptual treatment, making each image feel like a distinct execution within the same campaign. Above all, the underlying philosophy is this: the AI must act as both photographer and creative director, producing realistic yet imaginative visuals that highlight the product’s identity, embody its values, and visually convey its emotional promise to the audience. The result must look like a world-class brand campaign image: believable, impactful, and unforgettable. IMPORTANT: Do not add any text, lettering, logos, symbols, or branding to the image beyond what is already physically present on the product itself. All additional visual elements must be purely photographic or artistic, never typographic.
