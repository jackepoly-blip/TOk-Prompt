Analyze the uploaded book cover or document design with meticulous precision, preserving every element—typography, imagery, layout, spacing, proportions, and brand colors—exactly as it appears. The design identity is sacred and must never be distorted, cropped, recolored, or reinterpreted. The book must appear as a premium printed object, with full-bleed design wrapping seamlessly over the cover, spine, and edges, as if professionally produced. Render it with realistic weight, thickness, and surface qualities—smooth matte for elegance, glossy for sleek modernity, or soft-touch textures for a refined tactile impression. Then, instead of showing it from the viewer’s own POV, generate a photorealistic scene in which the Ideal Customer Avatar (ICA)—a natural, relatable person who represents the target audience—is holding and presenting the book. The ICA should not resemble or derive from the uploaded design but instead look exactly like the kind of customer who would be proud to own, use, or showcase this book. Their posture, gestures, and expressions should convey enthusiasm, trust, and satisfaction, making the book feel aspirational and highly valuable. The ICA’s hands, arms, and body language must look authentic and proportionate, never stiff or artificial, and always secondary in attention to the book itself. The book must remain tack-sharp, perfectly legible, and dominant in the composition, always the hero of the image. The environment around the ICA should be softly blurred but thoughtfully chosen to reinforce the promise of the resource. For example, a professional business guide might be shown in the hands of a polished executive-type ICA with a blurred office or desk scene behind them, complete with muted luxury cues. A creative workbook might be held by an artistic, vibrant ICA in a softly blurred studio setting with faint color fields and creative tools. A wellness or lifestyle book might be in the hands of a calm, approachable ICA with soft light, greenery, or cozy lifestyle details in the background. Lighting must sculpt the book and ICA naturally and atmospherically: soft daylight for approachable, positive resources; bold directional light for authoritative, impactful content; or moody cinematic tones for deep, transformative works. Depth of field should keep the ICA and the book sharply defined, with the background blurred into pleasing tones and textures that support but never distract. Variations are essential to make the results dynamic and premium: sometimes the ICA holds the book proudly upright and centered, sometimes angled as if mid-use, sometimes relaxed at their side, sometimes partly open to reveal interior spreads, always feeling intentional and natural. Post-processing should unify the scene with subtle finishing touches: refined contrast curves, clarity sharpening, soft bloom on highlights, gentle vignette, and optional fine grain for realism. The final image must feel like a premium lifestyle photo campaign, positioning the ICA as the proud owner of a high-value, beautifully produced book, and making the resource feel aspirational, desirable, and credible.
