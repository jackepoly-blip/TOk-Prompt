## Prompt

How to Get the Best Results with the Branded Mockup Prompt
1. Upload the right screenshots
Stick to clean digital screenshots (website, app, product UI).
Avoid messy backgrounds or photos of screens.
If you want multiple, combine them first on a single canvas (Google Drawings or Canva) and export as PNG.
2. Let the AI pick the device
The model will usually detect if it’s desktop, mobile, or tablet.
Don’t fight it... test with different screenshots and see which device it chooses naturally.
3. Expect variety
Each run should give you different angles and setups.
Try 3–4 generations and pick your favorite.
Look for zoomed-in details, wider setups, or POV shots.
4. Refine with short tweaks
If you don’t like the vibe, add a quick note:
“minimalist background”
“inside an office”
“flat lay on a desk”
Keep tweaks simple. One extra phrase is often enough.
5. Protect the screenshot
Double-check the output to make sure the screenshot itself hasn’t been cropped or distorted.
If it looks off, rerun the prompt instead of trying to fix inside the same thread.
6. Control the size
Remember: the output inherits the ratio of your last uploaded image.
If you need square or vertical, set that up first in Google Drawings/Canva.
7. Treat results as drafts
Not every run will be perfect.
Pick the best outputs, upscale them if needed, and save those as your polished assets.
THE PROMPT ​
Generate an image following these instructions: Analyze the subject in the attached image and generate one single, complete, final image as the output. Before generating the image, determine all creative variables up front, including device type, environment, lighting style, camera angle, composition, depth of field, and brand mood. Once these decisions are made, render the image as one cohesive, finished photograph. Do not show multiple options, stages, panels, or partial compositions. The result must be one unified visual. Begin by analyzing the uploaded screenshot or screenshots with meticulous precision. Identify whether the design belongs to a desktop interface, mobile phone screen, tablet, or another digital device category. Extract the screenshot content exactly as it appears. Proportions, layout, typography, spacing, imagery, and colors must be preserved with absolute fidelity. The screenshot’s identity is inviolable and must remain perfectly intact. It must not be cropped, stretched, recolored, stylized, filtered, blurred, or artistically reinterpreted. Any adjustment made should serve only to place the screenshot naturally within a real-world device frame while keeping every pixel sharp, legible, and unchanged. Next, select the single most contextually accurate device that would realistically display this interface, such as a phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop monitor. The device proportions must match the screenshot’s true aspect ratio so the screen content fits naturally and convincingly. The screenshot should feel physically present within the device display, with correct scale, edge alignment, reflections, and brightness so it appears genuinely rendered on the screen rather than layered on top. Once the device and screenshot placement are resolved, construct a surrounding environment that expresses the brand mood, message, and visual identity implied by the screenshot itself. The scene must be designed intentionally, guided by the design language, tone, and emotional cues found within the interface. The environment should feel believable and cohesive, supporting the product’s positioning without overpowering it. The screenshot remains the hero at all times. Choose one clear compositional strategy for the image and execute it fully. This may involve close framing, wide framing, or a dynamic angle, but only one approach should be used per image. Avoid mixing multiple perspectives or visual concepts within the same frame. Any human presence should remain secondary and understated, appearing only as partial or abstract elements when appropriate, ensuring attention stays on the screen content. Lighting must be selected deliberately to support the screenshot’s design character and brand tone. Light direction, intensity, color temperature, reflections, and shadows must be consistent across the device, screen, and environment so the scene reads as physically real. Color grading should unify the image while keeping the screenshot’s colors exact and undistorted. Finalize the image with restrained, professional post-processing that enhances realism and cohesion. Apply subtle tonal refinement, depth, contrast, and texture so the result feels like a high-end branded photograph created for a campaign or portfolio. The final output must read as one intentional, polished visual that could exist in a real-world marketing context. The end result must be a single, complete image that feels deliberate, premium, and cohesive, with all creative decisions resolved before rendering and no visual ambiguity about what the image represents.
